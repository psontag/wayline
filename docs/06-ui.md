# UI

Desktop first. Phones get a viewing-first layout.

Mockups are in `design/` (see `design/README.md`).

Every side panel and phone sheet follows "Side panels" in `07-design-system.md`.

## Screens

| Screen | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Trip list | Own trips and trips shared with me. Buttons for a new trip and for a GPX import. Phones show only the import, since New trip is in the top bar. Empty state with a short hint and the new trip button. |
| New trip | Where: dates and a start on the map. Trip: length, shape and parameters. |
| Import GPX | Turn GPX files into a trip. |
| Proposals | Compare generated trips and pick one. |
| Trip editor | View and edit a trip. Main screen. |
| Shared view | Trip editor without editing. |
| Settings | Theme and language. |

## New trip

Two steps on the same map:

| Step | Panel | Next |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Where | Days: 16-day forecast and trip length. Train from home. Areas. Signed routes. | Click an area: Area. Without areas, click a station: start set, go to Trip. "Ride n days of …": Proposals. "Choose a place": go to Trip. |
| 2 · Area | Weather, Train from home, Start, Trip, Signed routes of one area | "Show trips": Proposals. "More settings": Trip. "All areas": Where. |
| 3 · Trip | The form below, dates carried over | "Find routes": Proposals. Back: Where. |

New trip opens on Where. Back from the proposals opens Area for trips from an area, Trip for others, or Where with the route selected for a stretch of a signed route. Edit on the proposals opens Trip. The step and the area are in the URL (`?step=area&area=J16`).

Days:

- The 16 forecast days from today. Each day has a bar: the share of areas within the max time that are dry, have showers or are wet.
- The trip days are marked. A click on a day moves the first day there.
- Chips for 2, 3, 4 and 5 days. "Other dates" opens the first day and the number of days, for one day, longer trips or later dates.
- Days more than 10 days out are faded, here and in the area rows.
- Dates past the forecast say so under the strip.

Suggestions:

- Under Days, with a forecast. The three best windows of the trip length, not overlapping.
- Score: the areas within the max time that are dry on every day of the window. Each counts `1 / (1 + hours by train)`. Windows reaching more than 10 days out count 0.7 and say so.
- Each window: its dates, how many areas are dry, and the three fastest of them with their train time.
- Click a window: the dates move there. Click an area: the dates move there and the Area step opens.
- When no area is dry on enough days in a row, the section says so.

Train from home:

- Train time from home on the first day, in bands up to 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 h. Over 6 h has no color.
- Filters: max time (2, 3, 4 or 6 h, default 4), changes (≤ 1 by default, or ≤ 2), IC and ICE (on by default; off uses regional trains only).
- Hovering a station shows its name, time, changes and R when an IC or ICE needs a bike reservation. The legend explains R in a tooltip.
- Only Karlsruhe Hbf has train times. Another home station hides the layer and says why.

Areas:

- The map shows Germany in the official tourism regions (`areas.json`), colored by the train time to the fastest station of each. Each has a label with name and time. Areas over the max time are faded. No station dots and no signed routes on the map yet.
- With a forecast, a "Train time / Weather" switch sits on the map. Weather colors each area within the max time dry, showers or wet: the worst of the trip days. Areas over the max time are grey.
- The section lists the areas within the max time: dry on the trip days first, then fastest. Each row: weather dot, name, changes, R, time and a strip of the 16 days. Wet areas have a muted name. Seven rows, then "Show all". The summary counts the dry areas.
- Hovering a row outlines the area on the map. Clicking a row or the area opens the Area step.
- Data without areas shows the stations of all of Germany instead, as dots.

Area:

- The map zooms to the area and shows its stations, colored by train time.
- Weather: the area's 16-day strip, trip days marked. The summary says dry, showers or wet on your days.
- Train from home: the fastest station and its time, and how many stations have a train.
- Start: "Any station" by default, then the stations, fastest first. Four rows, then "Show all". A click on a station, in the list or on the map, picks it and marks it on the map.
- Trip: shape (Any, Loop, One way) and km a day (50, 70 or 90). "More settings" opens Trip with the start and shape set.
- Signed routes: the route shelf, only routes with 10 km or more in the area.
- "Show trips" with a picked station: one search from it. With "Any station": the three fastest stations, or the two fastest when the shape is Any. Any searches a loop and a one way trip from each. The searches run one after another, and the best three proposals by score stay. Each card says its start and the train time there.

Signed routes (route shelf):

- Routes of 50 km or more with a station within the max train time (`route-stations.json`). Each row: length, tags (rivers & lakes, wine, nature, sights, flat, hilly) and the stations in reach. Six rows, then "Show all".
- Order: routes with a stretch first, then the most km between the first and last station in reach.
- Stretch: a start and an end station on the route, about the trip's km (days and km per day of the form, within 15 %). Either way along the route. The least train time out (day type of the first day) plus home (day type of the last day) wins. With a forecast, a stretch up to 30 min slower by train wins when it has less headwind.
- Hovering a row draws the route on the map. Selecting it draws the stretch, marks its stations, fades the others and fits the map. The panel shows the stretch, its km and the trains there and home.
- "Ride n days of …" opens the proposals with start, end and the route. The planner tries that route first and ranks proposals that follow it first. The "fewer days" hint stays off: the route is the detour.
- Without a stretch the row says so. Other dates or a longer train time can find one.
- With a forecast, a row with rain on its stretch has a muted title and names the rainy days. The order stays the same. The panel shows rain and wind on the selected stretch: mostly tailwind, headwind, crosswind or little wind.

Weather:

- Rain is the total from 9:00 to 18:00, wind the mean over those hours. Per day: dry below 0.5 mm, showers below 3 mm, wet from 3 mm.
- One grid of forecast cells over the areas within the max time, at most 150 cells. Without areas, over the stations within 3 h.
- An area's rain on a day is the worst cell inside it. A small area takes the nearest cell.
- Stations and stretches with a wet trip day are faded.
- No rain or wind layer on the Where map.

One form for every planned trip:

- Length: one day or several days.
- Shape: A to B or loop.

Inputs depend on the choice:

| Input | When |
|---|---|
| Start | Always. A town or a station. Towns come first in the search and show their station if it is within 2 km. |
| End | A to B. Optional on several days: "Any station" lets the search pick where the trip ends. |
| Date and start time | Always. Filled in by default, since weather, hotels and arrival times need it. |
| Last day and back-by time | Several days. The days follow from the two dates. |
| Distance, max ascent | One day |
| Km per day, max ascent per day, hotel budget | Several days |

A day strip shows one tile per day with its target km. A later start makes day 1 shorter ("From 13:00"), an earlier back-by time the last day ("Back by 13:00").

## Import GPX

One GPX file per day. The days are sorted by file name and can be dragged into another order, also with a finger. The map shows them like stages in the editor. On phones it sits above the days once there are files.

| Input | Use |
|---|---|
| Name | Filled in from the start and end towns |
| Start date and start time | Optional. Dates and weather. |
| Hotel budget | Picks the hotel at each stop. Several days only. |
| Profile | Routes gaps between days and later edits |

Notes per day:

- A day that starts more than 200 m from the last stop gets the gap routed.
- A file without elevation takes it from the route network. Off the network, it is interpolated. The day gets a note when more than 2 km are off the network.

The result is a normal trip. Each stop is the end of a track, locked, named after the nearest town. Surface comes from the route network where the track runs along it, else it is unknown.

## Proposals

```
┌──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
│                              │ Routing 3 of 5 Cancel│
│                              ├──────────────────────┤
│     Map with all proposals,  │ 1  4 days · 312 km   │
│     numbered. Unselected     │    2,100 m · 85% paved│
│     ones are dimmed.         ├──────────────────────┤
│                              │ 2  ...               │
│                              ├──────────────────────┤
│                              │ 3  ...               │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

- Cards appear as the search finds them, sorted best first. A progress line and a Cancel button sit on top.
- The first card is selected while the search runs, until the user picks one. A picked card stays in place when better ones come in above it.
- Each card shows a bar and a row per day. Short days get a "Half day" or "Short day" tag.
- Card titles: "Direct" on one-day A to B. Else the signed route the proposal follows, on the first card that follows it, with "to <end>" without a chosen end. Later cards on that route show it as a tag. Else "To <end>" without a chosen end, longer or scenic on one-day A to B, else "Via <town>", with a town no earlier card is named after.
- Tags sit under the stats. Every card shows the first 3 and "+n" for the rest. "+n" lists the rest on hover.
- The main signed routes of a proposal, up to 2, come last as tags. A route already in the title or the followed-route tag is left out.
- Tag order: Few beds, followed route, Longer, Shorter, More climbing, forecast, then the clear winners, then the rarest route tags.
- A tag shows on hover what it means.
- A proposal that rides more than half of a signed route, for at least 30 % of its km, has a tag with the route's name.
- Route tags come from the planner (`05-trip-generation.md`). A tag more than half the cards have is hidden, Longer and Shorter too. More climbing always shows.
- Winners go to one proposal, by a clear margin: Flattest, Most paved, Shortest, Fastest, Least headwind. Shortest replaces Fastest on the same proposal.
- Forecast tags, on dated trips: Rain or Rain on day n when a day is wet (1 mm/h or more), Dry when all days are and not every card is, Tailwind, Headwind.
- Proposals are numbered on the map. Unselected ones are dimmed. The highlighted one is drawn day by day in alternating colors with numbered stops.
- Hover a card to highlight the route.
- Opening a proposal creates a draft trip. Going back shows the same proposals.
- No result: say which limit failed and offer to relax it.

## Trip editor

```
┌──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
│                              │ Trip name        ... │
│                              ├──────────────────────┤
│                              │ ▣ Dates    12–15 Jun │
│           Map                │ ▣ Trip     totals    │
│                              │ ▣ Days     day cards │
│                              │ ▣ Trains             │
├──────────────────────────────┤                      │
│ Elevation or weather         │                      │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

The panel follows "Side panels" in `07-design-system.md`. It has two levels:

| Level | Header | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| Trip | Trip name, trip menu (rename, duplicate, delete) | Dates (start date with headwind on the two days before and after, wind tips; the list stays put while picking), Trip (totals, surface), Days (one card per day with weather and hotel), Trains (timetables to the start and back, looked up when opened) |
| Day | All days, day chips, date, stop, stats, Changed | Getting there (first day), Ride (via points, surface, weather, climbs), Stop (lock, stop table), Hotel (hotel list, Booking.com), Destination (last day of one-way trips and routes: other train stops near the end, found on request, also on the map), Getting home (last day) |

`?stage=<n>` in the URL opens a day. Without it, the panel shows the trip and the map shows all days.

Top bar: save state, undo/redo, share, GPX export, settings.

### Interactions

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Select stage | Click card or route on map. Map zooms to the stage. |
| Add via point | Mouse: drag the line of the selected day. A routed preview follows the pointer, with the change in km and climbing next to it. Esc cancels. Touch: tap the line to place the point (see below). The point snaps to the route network. A ride out and back to it is cut off. If the best route stays the same, a note says so. |
| Route via a spot | Right-click the map, or press and hold with a finger. "Route via here" shows how far the spot is off the route and opens the pin there. |
| Select via point | Click or tap it, on the map or in the Ride section. It shows Remove above it, and Move on touch. Click the map or press Esc to close. |
| Move via point | Mouse: drag it, with a routed preview. Touch: select it, then Move. |
| Remove via point | Select it, then Remove or the Delete key. Or the remove button in the Ride section. The note after it offers Undo. |
| Undo, redo | Top bar, or ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y on other systems). |
| Move stop | Pick one of the suggested towns. The route stays and splits at the new stop. The stop gets locked. |
| Revert a day | A changed day shows "Changed" with a Revert button. It restores route, stop and hotel as proposed. The next day follows. |
| Lock stop | Lock button in the Stop section. |
| Change hotel | Hotel list for the stop: name, stars if known, distance to route. |
| Booking.com | One link per stop with dates, budget and 8+ reviews. Per hotel: a Booking search around it, and its website if OSM has one and it still works. |
| Saved hotels | A bookmark on hotel rows and map cards. Saved hotels stay in view at their stop. |
| Trains | bahn.de links at start and end stations. |
| Change date | Edit start date. Dates and weather update. |
| Export | Whole trip, or one stage. |

The gesture follows the input, not the screen size: a mouse or trackpad drags, a finger or pen taps. An iPad gets the desktop layout with touch gestures.

Placing a via point on touch:

- A pin stays in the middle of the map. You move the map under it.
- The routed preview and the change in km and climbing follow.
- A bar at the bottom of the map has Cancel and Done. Nothing saves before Done.
- On phones the sheet goes down while placing and comes back after.

- Changes save automatically.
- After an edit, a short note shows downstream changes, e.g. "Day 3 now ends in Lichtenfels".
- A changed day shows the proposed route as a soft grey line on the map and a grey area in the elevation profile. Only the part that differs shows, and only for the selected day.
- In the profile, the proposed part is stretched to fit between the points where the routes split and meet again, so the rest of the day lines up.
- Hovering the "Changed" notice or the "Proposed route" legend shows the proposed route stronger in both places. The info line then shows its distance and ascent.
- While derived data loads, old values stay visible and are marked as updating.

### Elevation and weather

- Elevation and weather share one km axis.
- Hover shows a crosshair and a marker on the map.
- The elevation line is one neutral color. A "Surface" strip below the km axis shows the surface, with its legend under it.
- Climbs are filled under the line, colored by grade: under 4 %, 4–7 %, over 7 %. The legend sits above the chart. Wide climbs show their gain.
- With the crosshair on a climb, that climb shows stronger in the chart, on the map and in the climb list.
- The highest point is labelled.
- On proposal cards, a short line from the profile down marks where each day starts.
- Weather is shown for the expected time at each km.
- With no day open, Weather shows the whole trip along the km: feels-like temperature, rain, wind and gusts, with day breaks. The info line names the day and time at the pointer, and the map shows the weather of that moment.
- The stage card shows wind as a share of the distance (head, tail, cross) and the average wind speed. Below 10 km/h it says "Calm".
- The day card and day panel warn when the ride starts below 3 °C ("Cold start: −1 °C") and when the arrival, with breaks, is less than an hour before sunset ("Arrives 16:20, sunset 16:37").
- The proposal with clearly the least headwind (10 points ahead) gets a "Least headwind" tag, like Flattest or Shortest.
- The trip summary suggests riding the trip the other way round, or a start date up to two days earlier or later, when that cuts the headwind share by 20 points or more. Days below 10 km/h wind count as calm.
- Beyond the forecast range: "Forecast from <date>".

### Day trips with the wind

- New trip form: one day offers A to B, Loop and Anywhere. Anywhere rides in any direction and ends at a station.
- Day loops and Anywhere show a "With the wind" switch, on by default.
- Anywhere proposals are named "To <station>", or "<route> to <station>" when they follow a signed route. A ride from a station back to the start is "Home from <station>", with "Train out" in its subtitle.
- One-day cards show the wind with its speed: "Tailwind 21 km/h", "Headwind 18 km/h".
- With wind, the proposals map shows one arrow at the start: where the wind blows, colored by speed, with its speed.

## Phone layout

- Viewing first.
- Map with a bottom sheet at three heights: peek, half, full.
- Every map has a maximise button. It hides the panels and top bar until tapped again.
- New trip: Where shows a half-height map above the areas, then the dates. Trip shows a small map above the form once a start is chosen.
- The trip editor sheet shows the same trip and day levels as the desktop side panel. It opens on the trip level. Opening or switching a day sets the sheet to half and fits the day into the map above it.
- On the day level, Elevation and Weather sit right under the day header. On the trip level they sit at the end.
- The trip editor's floating top bar has back, trip name, Undo once there is something to undo, and a menu with Redo, Share and Export GPX.
- "Tap the route to add a via point" shows until the first via point is placed.
- Route edits use the touch gestures from the trip editor interactions.

## Accessibility

- Every map action also works from the side panel.
- Proposals are numbered, not marked by color only.
- Reduced motion is respected.
