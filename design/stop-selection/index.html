Choosing where a day ends, and which way it goes

Today the Stop section lists other towns on the same line. You pick one blind: the map does not show the new route, and every choice follows the path you already have. Below are five ways to fix that, from a small change to a bigger one.

Day being edited Next day Current route

Example: day 2, Bamberg to Coburg, 72 km. Day 3 goes on to Kulmbach. Maps are sketches, numbers are made up.

Option A

Preview before you pick

Same list of stops. Hover or tap a stop and the map draws this day and the next day as they would be. The list shows the change in km and climbing for both days. Apply or Cancel.

Pros

  • Small change. The routes are already computed for the km numbers (useStopOptions), they just aren't drawn.
  • Shows what happens to the next day, which is often the real cost of a choice.
  • Works the same on phone: tap a row, see the map, confirm.

Cons

  • Still only towns on the current line. Doesn't solve "I want to go a different way".
  • Adds a confirm step where today one tap is enough.
Effort: small. Preview layer on the map plus deltas in StopChoices.
Option B

Sleep anywhere in reach

Instead of towns on the line, the map shades the band you can reach today, from about 15 % shorter to 20 % longer than your target. Every town with beds in that band is a choice, also off the current route. Pick one and the day and the next day are routed fresh.

Day 2 · Sat 14 Jun

Coburg72 km · 540 m · 21 hotels · now
Weismain78 km · 620 m · 4 hotels
Seßlach66 km · 450 m · 3 hotels
Day 3 then: 58 km, −14 km
End in Lichtenfels

Pros

  • Matches how you think on tour: "where can I sleep tonight?"
  • Big changes become one click: a stop off the line pulls the whole day onto a new path.
  • Bed counts on the map make "is this a real town" obvious.

Cons

  • Needs a route per candidate. 5–10 router calls per open day; fine on a dev box, slower on BRouter.
  • Changes two days at once, and may wreck day 3. Needs the preview from A to be safe.
  • The band is distance as the crow flies; real km can fall outside. Rows show routed km once known.
  • The route to the new stop is "cheapest", not your trip's flavour (scenic, hilly), unless we route with the trip's profile.
Effort: medium. Candidate search from the place index, routing, band layer.
Option C

Other ways to the same stop

Keep the stop. A "Other ways" button runs the generator for just this day and shows up to three routes that really differ, drawn like the proposals map. Each card says what it is: valley, hills, gravel, along a signed route.

Use Main valley

Pros

  • Answers "different path" directly. Choices differ by corridor, not by a few hundred metres.
  • Reuses the generator's flavours, corridor check and tags, and the proposals look people already know.
  • Nothing else in the trip moves. Low risk.

Cons

  • The stop is fixed, so a day can't grow or shrink much.
  • Needs the generator to run for one stage (start and end given, no cutting). Some work in packages/generator.
  • In thin networks you get one or two ways, or none. Needs an empty state.
Effort: medium. Stage-only generator entry, a panel section, the proposals map layer.
Option D

Change this day: where and which way

B and C together in one "Change day" mode. Step 1: pick a stop in the band. Step 2: pick one of the ways there. A strip of day bars shows what the change does to the rest of the trip before you apply it.

Pros

  • One place for every mid-size change: shorter, longer, other town, other path.
  • The trip strip makes knock-on effects visible before you commit.
  • Via points stay for fine tuning; this covers the big moves.

Cons

  • Most UI and most compute. A second mode in the editor to learn.
  • Two steps on a phone bottom sheet get cramped.
  • Only worth it once A, B and C each work on their own.
Effort: large. Best reached by building A, then C, then B, and merging them.
Option E

Re-plan from here

For the big change: keep days 1 and 2, then ask the generator for new proposals for the rest of the trip, starting at tonight's stop and ending where the trip ends. Locked stops stay. The result opens in the proposals view; pick one and it replaces the later days.

Re-plan days 3–5
From Coburg to Kulmbach in 3 days. Days 1–2 stay.
Direct3 days · 186 km · 1 900 m
Frankenwald3 days · 214 km · 3 100 m
Jura and Main3 days · 198 km · 1 400 m
Use Direct

Pros

  • The only option that changes several days at once in a sane way.
  • Almost no new UI: it's the proposals screen with a fixed start and fewer days.
  • Good after a real-world change: weather turns, a day was lost, you want to go home a day early.

Cons

  • Throws away hand edits on later days, unless they are locked.
  • A loop needs "back to the start" as the end; a route (track) trip can't use it at all.
  • Heavy: a full generator run, seconds not milliseconds.
Effort: medium. Generator already takes start and end; mostly wiring and the replace step.

Recommendation

Build A now, then C, then B. Merge into D only if it feels needed.

  • A first. It fixes the blind pick with data we already have. Every later option needs the preview anyway.
  • C next. It gives the "really different path" you asked for, with the least risk: one day changes, nothing else moves.
  • B after. Stops off the line. With A's preview in place, the two-day change is safe.
  • E is a separate, cheap win for "the plan broke". It can come at any time.

What else

What's missing around this

Things that would make the choice easier, roughly by value.

Compare

Show the cost of a choice

Deltas for km, climbing, gravel share and arrival time, for this day and the next. Today you see km only.

Stop quality

Why this town

Hotels in budget and the cheapest price, a station for bail-out, a shop or restaurant open on a Sunday. "7 hotels" alone doesn't say much.

Weather

Wind and rain per choice

A longer day with tailwind can beat a short one into a headwind. Ways in C can be tagged "tailwind" or "rain after 15:00".

Shaping

Avoid this part

Select a stretch on the map and say "not here". The day re-routes around it. Faster than dragging three via points.

Shaping

Must pass

Pick a town, sight or café the day has to go through. A via point by name, with search, instead of by dragging.

Trip

Day balance

A small bar chart of all days. Moving one stop can offer to even out the unlocked ones after it.

Trip

Split, merge, rest day

Turn one long day into two, two short days into one, or add a rest day at a stop. Common changes with no button today.

Safety net

Undo history

Revert only goes back to the proposal. Trying options is easier with undo and redo for each step.

Arrival

Arrival time and daylight

"Arrive around 17:30, sunset 21:40" on each choice, from start time and average speed, which we already have.
