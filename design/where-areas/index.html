Where step: days, area, station

All of Germany in about 40 named areas, coloured by train time from Karlsruhe up to 6 h. The day strip (W1) and suggested windows (W4) sit on top. Pick an area to zoom in and see its stations. Try it: click days, suggestions, tiles, rows and the max time. The data is made up.

How it works

Rules behind the mockup

Areas
  • About 40 regions people know, hand-drawn once, stored as a small GeoJSON.
  • Train time of an area: the fastest station in it.
  • Weather of an area: forecast cells inside it, worst value of the day (09–18).
  • Riding quality (#341) can join as one more value per area later.
Train time up to 6 h
  • Max time: 2, 3, 4 or 6 h. Default 4 h.
  • Five bands: 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 h. Areas over the max are faded, not hidden, so you see the whole country.
  • Needs two new tokens, reach-4 and reach-5, and a new ramp (table below).
  • Pipeline: reach from Karlsruhe out to 6 h, IC/ICE with bike reservation.
Days and suggestions
  • Strip: share of areas within the max time that are dry, with showers, or wet.
  • Suggestions: the best windows of the trip length. Score: areas within the max time that are dry on every day, closer ones count more.
  • Each suggestion names its closest dry areas. Click one to pick it.
  • Days after day 10 are faded.
Area picked
  • Map zooms to the area. Only its stations show, coloured by train time.
  • Panel: the area's 16 days, stations, signed routes in the area.
  • Picking a station goes to Trip, as today.
  • Rainy areas in the window are faded and hatched, like stations today.
