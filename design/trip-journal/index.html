Brainstorm · #165 Overview map for trips

All trips on one map, and a diary of the ones you rode

Three layouts for the overview map, then the smaller choices inside it: line colors, how a trip becomes "ridden", filters, and what else could make it feel like a diary. The sketches use example trips around Franconia. Hover a trip in a panel to highlight it on the map.

What #165 asks for

All trips on a mapEvery trip from the trip list, each in its own color.
List in the side panelSame trips on the right, with useful filters.
Planned vs. riddenA flag for trips you actually rode.
Set the date laterGive a ridden trip its real date after the fact.
A diaryThe whole thing should read like a log of your trips.
Decided
  • Journal is its own screen. Its panel is option A's: Filters, one section per year, Planned, Shared with me.
  • Lines: one color per trip, planned trips dashed.
  • "Ridden" is renamed to Completed, since it applies to the whole trip.
  • Extras in the first version: year totals, notes, the "Did you complete this trip?" notice, and the My rides layer.
  • Trips shared with you show too.

The full plan is in issue #165. The rest of this page is the original brainstorm.

Recommendation

Option A: a Map view on the trip list, with the ridden trips grouped by year from option B. One screen, one list of trips, two ways to look at it. Lines in one color per trip, planned trips dashed. "Ridden" lives in the Dates section of the trip editor and reuses the start date.

Line colors

With many trips on one map, color has one job: tell lines apart and link each line to its row in the list.

Per trip Recommended

A fixed palette of 7–8 colors, picked from the trip id so a trip keeps its color. Planned trips dashed. The list shows the same color as a dot. Colors repeat past 8 trips; hover and the dot still link them.

By status

Ridden in the route color, planned in grey and dashed. Clearest for "where have I been", but trips that touch melt into one line.

By year

Older years fade to grey, the current year in peach. Good for the diary in option B. Same problem as status for trips of one year.

New tokens: trip-1 to trip-8. The first five can reuse the proposal colors (route-best … route-sights); three more are needed.

Marking a trip as ridden

The Dates section of a trip gets a choice: Planned or Ridden. Ridden needs a date, and that date is just the start date. Setting a date in the past is how you add a trip you rode long ago.

  • The same section on the overview map's trip level and in the trip editor.
  • Once the last day of a planned trip is past, the panel shows a notice: "Did you ride this?" with Mark as ridden.
  • A ridden trip with a past date shows no forecast and no "Forecast from". Later: real weather from the archive.
  • Riding the trip the other way round, or on other days, is a different trip. Keep it simple: duplicate and edit.
  • Importing a GPX of a past ride offers Ridden right away.

Data: add ridden: boolean to Trip and TripSummary. Ridden requires startDate. No new date field.

Filters

Few, and all as chips in one folded section. The summary says what is on, so a folded section still tells you.

FilterValuesWhy
StatusAll, Planned, RiddenThe main split from the issueFirst

LengthOne day, Several daysDay rides and tours look very different on a mapFirst
Only in viewSwitch: list shows only trips visible on the map"What did I ride around here?"Later

Filters live in the URL (?status=ridden&year=2026), like ?stage= in the editor.
