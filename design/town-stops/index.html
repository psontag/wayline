Trip editor · day stops

Pick any town as a day's stop

Towns with more than 8 hotels show on the map. Hover one to see how today and tomorrow change. Click it to make it the stop.

The maps below work: hover and click the towns, flip the legend switch. Towns, positions and numbers are made up. Day 3 runs from Friedrichshafen to Wangen, day 4 from Wangen to Füssen.

What I understand

  • A new map layer for the selected day: every town within 50 km of the day's stop (dashed circle) with more than 8 hotels within the hotel radius (HOTEL_KM). A badge with the hotel icon shows the count.
  • The layer shows next to the stop alternatives we have now.
  • A switch in the map legend turns the layer on and off.
  • Hover a town: the map shows the day ending there and the next day starting there, like the stop preview today. The old route dims.
  • The km and ascent change shows while you hover, in a dark label like the one when you drag the route.
  • Click: the town becomes the stop. Today and tomorrow are routed again. The hotel picks the nearest hotel, as it does for alternatives now.
  • Moving a stop changes two days. So the label shows both days, not just one.
Option A

Hotel badges and a cursor label

Closest to the route drag. Badges show hotels. The label next to the pointer has both days.

Good

  • Same pattern as dragging the route. Nothing new to learn.
  • All numbers are where you look.
  • Small change: one layer, one label, the existing preview line.

Not so good

  • You only learn which towns fit by hovering each one.
  • No hover on phones. Needs its own flow (see below).
Option B

Short label, numbers in the panel

The label only says the town and its hotels. The Route section of the side panel shows the day as it would be: km and ascent, today and tomorrow, before and after.

Good

  • Room for more: arrival time, km per day against the trip's target, hotels in budget.
  • The same panel works on the phone sheet.

Not so good

  • Eyes jump between map and panel.
  • The panel content flickers while the pointer moves over the map.
Option C

Badges colored by fit

Same label as A. The badge color says how well both days fit the trip's km and ascent per day, before you hover. The legend can switch the badge between hotels and today's km.

Good

  • You see good stops at a glance. Hover only to confirm.
  • Shows why a big town is a bad stop: it makes tomorrow too long.

Not so good

  • Needs km and ascent for every town up front. A crow-flies estimate first, routed numbers on hover.
  • One more color code on the map.
For all options

On the phone: tap to preview, then confirm

Tap a town to show the preview and a sheet with the numbers. "Stop here" changes it. Tap the map to cancel.

Decided

  • Only for the selected day. Not in the whole-trip view.
  • Towns within 50 km of the day's current stop.
  • Shows next to the current alternatives markers.
  • On the last day it can move the trip's end, only for A to B trips, like the train stops today.
  • Hotel count as a badge with the hotel icon.
  • Hover routes on demand. Each hover is two routing calls, cached per town. A short delay keeps fast mouse moves cheap.
