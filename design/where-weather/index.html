Weather on the area map, and the way to proposals

Four ways to show weather once Germany is split into areas, none with a rain overlay. Below the mockup: three flows from picking an area to seeing trips. The outlines and train times are the real data from Karlsruhe. The weather is made up.

Try it: click days, trip length, max time. Pick an area on the map or in the list, then “Show trips”.

From an area to proposals

What it needs

Work behind the chosen flow

Weather per area
  • Worst forecast cell in the area, 09:00–18:00, for all 16 days.
  • One grid over the areas in reach. A small area takes the nearest cell.
  • The rain and wind layer goes from the Where map.
  • A “Train time / Weather” switch on the map colours the areas by one or the other.
Area step
  • New panel: weather, train, start, signed routes, trip.
  • Trip settings move from the Trip form into a short “Trip” section with defaults.
  • The full form stays behind “More settings”.
Proposals for an area
  • The generator takes up to three start stations: the fastest ones by train.
  • Each proposal says where it starts and how long the train takes.
  • A picked station still limits it to one start.
